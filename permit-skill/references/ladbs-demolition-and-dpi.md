- Jurisdiction: City of Los Angeles

- Last Updated: February 2026 (compiled from LADBS official sources)

## Demolition Permit Types

### Small / Partial Demolition
- Removing interior non-bearing walls, detached garages, sheds, patio covers, fences
- May qualify as **express permit** via [PermitLA](https://www.permitla.org) — no full plan review required
- Standard LADBS inspection at completion
- DPI generally **NOT required**

### Full Demolition
- Complete razing of an entire building or structure
- Requires standard **plan check** through LADBS ePlanCheck
- Multiple clearances required before permit issuance
- DPI may be required depending on structure type and scope

## Hazardous Materials Requirements

### Asbestos (SCAQMD Rule 1403)
- **Mandatory** asbestos survey for all structures built before 1978
- SCAQMD must be notified **10 working days** before demolition begins
- Proper abatement must be completed before any demolition
- SCAQMD website: https://www.aqmd.gov

### Lead Paint
- Lead paint evaluation required for pre-1978 structures
- Must follow Cal/OSHA and EPA RRP (Renovation, Repair, Painting) Rule

## Registered Deputy Inspector (DPI) Requirements

### What is a DPI?
A Registered Deputy Inspector is a professional certified by LADBS to perform **continuous or special inspections** on construction projects on behalf of the Department. They are employed and paid by the property owner (not the city).

### When DPI is Required for Demolition
- Multi-story structural demolition
- Demolition affecting lateral force-resisting systems
- Demolition requiring shoring or underpinning of adjacent structures
- Hazardous material removal (separate DOSH/SCAQMD requirements also apply)
- Hillside demolition with grading implications

### When DPI is NOT Required
- Interior non-bearing wall removal
- Detached garage or shed demolition (small structures)
- Patio cover or fence removal
- Small/partial demolition with no structural impact

### DPI Classifications (LADBS)

| Code | Classification |
|------|---------------|
| RC | Reinforced Concrete |
| SM | Structural Masonry |
| SSW | Structural Steel / Welding |
| GD | Grading |
| SFRM | Sprayed Fire-Resistant Materials |
| MB | Methane Barrier |
| WD | Wood |

### Who to Contact About DPI Requirements
1. **Your assigned LADBS plan checker** — they flagged the DPI requirement and know which classification is needed
2. **LADBS Inspection Division** — (213) 473-3231 or 311
3. **LADBS Development Services Center** — 201 N. Figueroa St., 4th Floor, Los Angeles, CA 90012
4. **Your demolition contractor** — typically handles DPI coordination and has existing relationships with DPI firms

### DPI Process for a Demolition Permit
1. LADBS plan checker identifies DPI requirement during permit review
2. Owner/contractor hires a Registered Deputy Inspector with the correct LADBS classification
3. DPI performs continuous inspection during demolition work
4. DPI keeps records and submits reports to LADBS and the design professional of record
5. DPI files a **Certificate of Compliance** with LADBS upon completion
6. LADBS city inspector performs final sign-off

### DPI Certification Fees (for those seeking to BECOME a DPI)
- New exam application: **$528** per classification
- Renewal: **$482** per classification
- Separate fee for each additional classification
- No refunds after exam is administered
- Certificate valid for **3 years**, renewable

## Fire-Damaged Structures (2025 Wildfires)

LADBS issued **P-BC-2025-155** for demolition of structures destroyed by the 2025 wildfires:
- Expedited/modified permitting process
- Fee collection suspension for qualifying properties
- Applies to property owners who held ownership on or before January 7, 2025
- URL: https://dbs.lacity.gov/sites/default/files/efs/forms/pc17/P-BC-2025-155-Demolition-Final.pdf
