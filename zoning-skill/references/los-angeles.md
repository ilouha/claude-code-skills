# City of Los Angeles — Zoning Reference

Source: Los Angeles Municipal Code (LAMC), Chapter 1 — General Provisions and Zoning.
Official resource: [LA City Planning](https://planning.lacity.gov) | [ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org)

> **Note**: Los Angeles updated its zoning code structure in recent years. Always verify
> current regulations at planning.lacity.gov or through ZIMAS for parcel-specific info.

---

## Residential Zones

### RE (Residential Estate) Zones

**RE40** — Residential Estate Zone (min 40,000 sq ft lots)
- Purpose: Very low-density single-family residential, estate-style lots
- Permitted uses: Single-family dwelling, parks, farming/gardening, accessory buildings, ADUs
- Min lot area: 40,000 sq ft
- Max height: 36 ft (2 stories typical)
- Front setback: 25 ft
- Side setback: 10 ft (for lots over 70 ft wide), 5 ft (for narrower lots)
- Rear setback: 25 ft
- Density: 1 dwelling unit per 40,000 sq ft of lot area

**RE20** — Min lot area: 20,000 sq ft. Density: 1 du per 20,000 sq ft.
**RE15** — Min lot area: 15,000 sq ft. Density: 1 du per 15,000 sq ft.
**RE11** — Min lot area: 11,000 sq ft. Density: 1 du per 11,000 sq ft.
**RE9** — Min lot area: 9,000 sq ft. Density: 1 du per 9,000 sq ft.

### RS — Residential Suburban Zone

- Purpose: Low-density suburban single-family residential
- Min lot area: 7,500 sq ft | Min lot width: 65 ft
- Max height: 33 ft
- Setbacks: Front 20 ft, side 5 ft, rear 15 ft
- Density: 1 du per 7,500 sq ft
- Permitted: Single-family dwelling, ADU, home occupation, parks

### R1 — Single-Family Zone (One-Family Zone)

- **LAMC Section**: 12.08
- Purpose: Single-family residential neighborhoods
- **Permitted uses by right**:
  - One single-family dwelling per lot
  - Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) per state law (CA AB 68, SB 13)
  - Junior ADU (JADU) — conversion of existing space within the primary dwelling
  - Home occupation (limited commercial activity within the home)
  - Parks, playgrounds
  - Small family daycare (up to 8 children)
  - Accessory structures (garage, pool, shed)
  - Farming/gardening (non-commercial)
- **Conditionally permitted** (requires CUP or similar):
  - Large family daycare (9-14 children)
  - Religious institutions (churches, temples, etc.)
  - Schools
  - Public utility facilities
  - Wireless telecom facilities
- **Prohibited**:
  - Multi-family dwellings (duplexes, apartments) — *but see SB 9 below*
  - Commercial uses (retail, office, restaurant)
  - Industrial uses
- **Development Standards**:
  - Min lot area: 5,000 sq ft
  - Min lot width: 50 ft
  - Max height: 33 ft (or 2 stories in Hillside areas, sometimes 28 ft)
  - Front setback: 20 ft (or prevailing setback, whichever is less)
  - Side setback: 5 ft (combined 10 ft for interior lots)
  - Rear setback: 15 ft
  - FAR: 0.5:1 in the RFA (Residential Floor Area) district; varies otherwise
  - Max lot coverage: ~45-50% (depends on RFA applicability)
  - Parking: 2 covered spaces per dwelling unit
- **Key notes**:
  - California SB 9 (effective 1/1/2022) allows lot splits and duplexes on R1 lots
  - ADUs are allowed by right under state law regardless of local zoning
  - Hillside areas have additional restrictions
  - Many R1 areas have HPOZ (Historic Preservation Overlay Zone) overlays

### R2 — Two-Family Zone (Duplex Zone)

- **LAMC Section**: 12.09
- Purpose: Low-density residential allowing duplexes
- Permitted: Everything in R1 PLUS duplex, two SFDs on one lot, ADU + JADU
- Min lot area: 5,000 sq ft | Min lot width: 50 ft
- Max height: 33 ft
- Setbacks: Front 20 ft, side 5 ft, rear 15 ft
- Density: 1 du per 2,500 sq ft (so 5,000 sq ft lot = 2 units)
- Parking: 2 spaces per unit (4 total for a duplex)

### RD (Restricted Density Multiple Dwelling) Zones

- **RD6**: 1 du per 6,000 sq ft, max height 33 ft
- **RD5**: 1 du per 5,000 sq ft, max height 33 ft
- **RD4**: 1 du per 4,000 sq ft, max height 33 ft
- **RD3**: 1 du per 3,000 sq ft, max height 33 ft
- **RD2**: 1 du per 2,000 sq ft, max height 33 ft
- **RD1.5**: 1 du per 1,500 sq ft, max height 45 ft, setbacks: front 15 ft, side 5 ft, rear 15 ft — common for small apartment buildings

### R3 — Multiple Dwelling Zone

- **LAMC Section**: 12.10
- Purpose: Medium-density multi-family residential
- Permitted: All R2 uses PLUS apartments, condos, boarding houses
- Min lot area: 5,000 sq ft
- Density: 1 du per 800 sq ft | Max height: 45 ft
- Setbacks: Front 15 ft, side 5 ft, rear 15 ft

### R4 — Multiple Dwelling Zone

- **LAMC Section**: 12.11
- Purpose: High-density multi-family residential
- Density: 1 du per 400 sq ft | Max height: 75 ft (subject to Height District)
- Setbacks: Front 15 ft, side 5 ft (10+ ft for taller), rear 15 ft

### R5 — Multiple Dwelling Zone

- Purpose: Highest-density residential
- Density: 1 du per 200 sq ft | Max height: Unlimited (subject to Height District)
- Primarily found in Downtown LA

## Commercial Zones

### CR — Commercial Restricted
- Purpose: Low-intensity commercial at edge of residential
- Permitted: Office, limited retail, personal services, restaurants (no drive-through)
- Max height: 30-45 ft

### C1 — Limited Commercial Zone (LAMC 12.13)
- Purpose: Neighborhood-serving commercial
- Permitted: Retail, restaurants, offices, banks, medical, studios, churches
- Also permits R3-density residential (mixed-use)
- Conditionally permitted: Bars, auto repair, gas stations, drive-throughs
- Max height: 30-75 ft (per Height District)

### C1.5 — Limited Commercial
- C1 uses plus limited manufacturing incidental to retail
- Residential at R3 density

### C2 — Commercial Zone (LAMC 12.14)
- Purpose: General commercial — most common commercial zone in LA
- Permitted: All C1 uses PLUS auto sales/repair, hotels, theaters, gyms, wholesale
- Residential at R4 density
- Conditionally permitted: Nightclubs, adult entertainment, recycling centers
- Max height: Per Height District

### C4 — C2 uses minus certain nuisance uses. Residential at R4 density.
### C5 — Central commercial (Downtown LA). Most permissive. Residential at R5 density.
### CM — Commercial Manufacturing. Offices, warehousing, light manufacturing, studios. Residential typically NOT permitted unless overlay allows.

## Manufacturing Zones

### MR1 — Restricted Industrial. Light manufacturing, offices, studios. No residential.
### MR2 — Restricted Light Industrial. Broader manufacturing. No residential.
### M1 — Limited Industrial (LAMC 12.17.5). Light to medium industrial, warehousing, auto repair. No residential.
### M2 — Light Industrial. Medium manufacturing. No residential.
### M3 — Heavy Industrial. Heavy manufacturing. No residential. Rare — port/industrial areas.

## Key California State Law Overrides

### SB 9 (Housing Opportunity and More Efficiency Act)
- Effective: January 1, 2022
- Allows lot splits and duplexes on R1 lots
- Requirements: Urban area, not historic district, not VHFHSZ, owner-occupied (for lot split)
- Result: Up to 4 units on a formerly single-family lot

### ADU Laws (AB 68, SB 13, AB 881, AB 587)
- ADUs and JADUs allowed by right in all residential zones
- Cities cannot impose min lot sizes or owner-occupancy for ADUs
- One ADU + one JADU on single-family lots
- Multi-family: at least one ADU allowed plus conversion of non-habitable space

### SB 10 — Allows cities to voluntarily upzone for up to 10-unit buildings near transit (not self-executing)

### AB 2011 / SB 6 (2022) — Allows residential on commercial land with affordable housing component

### Builder's Remedy — If city lacks compliant Housing Element, developers can propose non-conforming projects with affordable units
