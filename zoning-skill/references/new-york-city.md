# New York City — Zoning Reference

Source: NYC Zoning Resolution (ZR).
Official resource: [NYC DCP](https://www.nyc.gov/site/planning/) | [ZoLa](https://zola.planning.nyc.gov)

---

## Residential Districts

### R1 — Detached Single-Family
- Max FAR: 0.50 | Min lot: 9,500 sq ft | Min width: 100 ft
- Max lot coverage: 35% | Max height: 35 ft
- Setbacks: Front 20 ft, side min 5 ft each (total 13 ft), rear 30 ft
- Parking: 1 per unit

### R2 — Detached Single-Family (Medium Lots)
- Max FAR: 0.50 | Min lot: 5,700 sq ft | Min width: 40 ft
- Max lot coverage: 45% | Max height: 35 ft
- Setbacks: Front 15 ft, side two 5 ft, rear 30 ft

### R3 — General Residence (Low Density)
- Includes R3-1 (detached only), R3-2 (all types), R3A, R3X
- Max FAR: 0.50 | Max height: 35 ft

### R4 — General Residence (Low-Medium Density)
- Max FAR: 0.75 (R4), 0.90 (R4A) | Max height: 35 ft

### R5 — General Residence (Medium Density)
- Max FAR: 1.25 | Max height: 40 ft (R5D)

### R6 — General Residence (Medium-High Density)
- Max FAR: 2.43 (residential), 4.8 (community facility)
- R6A: FAR 3.0, height 70 ft | R6B: FAR 2.0, height 50 ft
- Parking: 70% of units | Common in Brooklyn and Queens

### R7 — General Residence (High Density)
- Max FAR: 3.44 (R7-1), 4.0 (R7-2)
- R7A: FAR 4.0, height 80 ft | R7B: FAR 3.0, height 75 ft
- Parking: 60% of units

### R8 — General Residence (High Density)
- Max FAR: 6.02 | R8A: FAR 6.02, height 120 ft | R8B: FAR 4.0, height 75 ft
- Parking: 40% of units | Upper East/West Side, Downtown Brooklyn

### R9 — Very High Density
- Max FAR: 7.52, up to 10.0 with Inclusionary Housing bonus
- R9A: height 135 ft | Parking: 40%

### R10 — Highest Density
- Max FAR: 10.0, up to 12.0 with IH bonus
- No fixed height limit | Parking: 40% (many exemptions)
- Midtown, Financial District

## Commercial Districts

### C1/C2 — Local/Neighborhood Commercial (overlay on residential)
- C1: Neighborhood retail | C2: Broader retail
- Residential at underlying district density | Commercial FAR: 1.0-2.0

### C4 — General Commercial (Major Retail)
- Department stores, large retail, offices, hotels | FAR: 3.4 to 10.0

### C5 — Restricted Central Commercial
- CBD offices, hotels | Residential at R10 equivalent | FAR: 4.0 to 15.0

### C6 — General Central Commercial
- Most permissive general commercial | FAR: 6.0 to 15.0

### C8 — General Service Commercial
- Auto-related, service businesses, warehousing | No residential | FAR: 1.0-2.0

## Manufacturing Districts

### M1 — Light Manufacturing
- Light industry, offices, warehousing | No residential (except MX) | FAR: 1.0-5.0

### M2 — Medium Manufacturing
- Heavier manufacturing, processing | No residential | FAR: 2.0-5.0

### M3 — Heavy Manufacturing
- Power plants, fuel storage, waste processing | No residential

---

## Key NYC Concepts

- **Contextual Districts** (A/B/D/X suffixes): Street wall and height rules for neighborhood character
- **MX Mixed-Use Districts**: Allow residential in M1 zones
- **Mandatory Inclusionary Housing (MIH)**: Requires affordable units in mapped areas
- **ULURP**: Required for rezonings and special permits
- **Air Rights Transfers**: Unused development rights transfer between adjacent lots
