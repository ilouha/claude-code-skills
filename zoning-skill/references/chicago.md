# City of Chicago — Zoning Reference

Source: Chicago Zoning Ordinance (Title 17 of the Municipal Code).
Official: [Zoning Map](https://gisapps.chicago.gov/ZoningMap/) | [Chicago DPD](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dcd.html)

---

## Residential Districts

### RS-1 — Residential Single-Unit (Detached, Lowest Density)
- Min lot: 6,250 sq ft | Width: 50 ft | FAR: 0.50 | Height: 30 ft
- Setbacks: Front 20 ft, side combined 6 ft, rear 28 ft | Parking: 1/unit

### RS-2 — Most Common Single-Family
- Min lot: 4,000 sq ft | Width: 33 ft | FAR: 0.65 | Height: 30 ft | Parking: 1/unit

### RS-3 — Single-Family (Dense Neighborhoods)
- Min lot: 2,500 sq ft | Width: 25 ft | FAR: 0.90 | Height: 30 ft

### RT-3.5 — Two-Flat
- Min lot: 2,500 sq ft | FAR: 1.05 | Height: 30 ft | Max 2 units | Parking: 1/unit

### RT-4 — Two-Flat (Taller)
- FAR: 1.20 | Height: 38 ft | Max 2 units

### RM-4.5 — Multi-Unit (Low Density)
- FAR: 1.20 | Height: 38 ft | Parking: 1/unit (0.5 near transit)

### RM-5 — Multi-Unit (Medium)
- FAR: 2.0 | Height: 45-50 ft | Common for 3-6 unit buildings

### RM-5.5 — FAR: 2.5 | Height: 55 ft
### RM-6 — FAR: 4.4 | Height: 65-70 ft | Parking: 0.50/unit near transit
### RM-6.5 — FAR: 6.6 | Height: 80+ ft | Lakefront and dense neighborhoods

## Special Districts

- **PMD** (Planned Manufacturing): Protected industrial areas
- **PD** (Planned Development): Custom site-specific zoning
- **DX** (Downtown Mixed-Use): Up to FAR 16.0 in DX-16
- **Chicago ADU Ordinance (2020)**: ADUs allowed in RS/RT/RM zones, owner-occupied, one per lot, no extra parking
