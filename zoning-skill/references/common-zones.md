# Common US Zoning Classifications — General Reference

**Important**: These are generalizations. Always verify the specific city's code.

---

## Residential Zones

- **R1 / R-1 / RS**: Single-family. One dwelling per lot. Min lot: 5,000-10,000 sq ft. Height: 30-35 ft.
- **R2 / R-2**: Two-family / duplex. Slightly higher density.
- **R3 / R-3 / RM**: Multi-family / medium density. Apartments, condos, townhouses. 15-30 units/acre.
- **R4 / R-4**: High-density multi-family. 30-60+ units/acre. Height: 50-100+ ft.
- **R5 / R-5**: Highest-density residential. Downtown areas. FAR and height controlled.

## Commercial Zones

- **C1 / B1**: Neighborhood commercial. Small retail, services. Often mixed-use residential above.
- **C2 / B2**: General commercial. The standard commercial zone. Retail, offices, restaurants.
- **C3 / B3**: Heavy / regional commercial. Big box, auto dealers. More auto-oriented.
- **C4 / C5**: Central business district. High-rise offices, hotels. FAR 10.0+.

## Manufacturing / Industrial Zones

- **M1 / I-1**: Light industrial. Manufacturing, warehousing, offices. No residential.
- **M2 / I-2**: General industrial. Broader manufacturing. No residential.
- **M3 / I-3**: Heavy industrial. Heaviest uses. No residential.

## Special Zones

- **PD / PUD**: Planned development. Custom zoning for large projects.
- **TOD**: Transit-oriented development overlay. Higher density, less parking near transit.
- **Historic District / HPOZ**: Design review overlay for historic areas.
- **A / AG**: Agricultural. Very low density, farming, rural uses.
- **OS**: Open space, parks, conservation.
