# IBC Occupancy Group Classifications — Reference

Source: International Building Code (IBC), Chapter 3 — Use and Occupancy Classification
IBC Sections 302–312

---

## Overview

Every building (or portion of a building) must be classified into one occupancy group
based on its use. The occupancy group determines fire-resistance, sprinkler, egress,
and many other requirements. There are 10 main groups with subgroups.

---

## Group A — Assembly

Buildings where people gather for civic, social, or recreational purposes.

### A-1 — Assembly with Fixed Seating (Performing Arts)
- **Uses**: Movie theaters, concert halls, TV studios with audience
- **Key threshold**: Fixed seating intended for viewing performances
- **IBC Section**: 303.1

### A-2 — Assembly for Food/Drink
- **Uses**: Restaurants, bars, nightclubs, banquet halls, casinos
- **Key threshold**: Food or drink consumption
- **IBC Section**: 303.2

### A-3 — Assembly for Worship, Recreation, or Other
- **Uses**: Churches, mosques, temples, community halls, bowling alleys, museums,
  galleries, libraries, lecture halls, courtrooms, gymnasiums
- **Key threshold**: Assembly uses not classified as A-1, A-2, A-4, or A-5
- **IBC Section**: 303.3
- **Notes**: This is the "catch-all" assembly group

### A-4 — Assembly for Viewing Indoor Sporting Events
- **Uses**: Arenas, indoor stadiums, skating rinks, swimming pools with spectator seating
- **IBC Section**: 303.4

### A-5 — Assembly for Outdoor Activities
- **Uses**: Stadiums, amusement park structures, bleachers, grandstands
- **IBC Section**: 303.5
- **Notes**: Outdoor — some requirements are relaxed

---

## Group B — Business

Buildings for office, professional, or service-type transactions.

- **Uses**: Offices, banks, government buildings (non-assembly), outpatient clinics,
  educational above 12th grade (colleges/universities), laboratories, professional
  services (barber, beauty, etc.)
- **IBC Section**: 304.1
- **Notes**: Doctors' offices and outpatient clinics that are NOT ambulatory surgical
  centers are Group B, not Group I

---

## Group E — Educational

Buildings used for education of children through 12th grade.

- **Uses**: K-12 schools, daycare with more than 5 children over 2.5 years old
- **IBC Section**: 305.1
- **Notes**: Daycare for 5 or fewer children = Group R-3. Daycare for children under
  2.5 years old = Group I-4. Universities and colleges = Group B.

---

## Group F — Factory/Industrial

Buildings for assembling, fabricating, manufacturing, or processing products.

### F-1 — Moderate Hazard
- **Uses**: Appliance manufacturing, furniture manufacturing, auto repair shops,
  woodworking, printing, dry cleaning, laundries
- **IBC Section**: 306.2

### F-2 — Low Hazard
- **Uses**: Beverage bottling, brick manufacturing, glass products, metal fabrication
  (noncombustible), food processing, ice manufacturing
- **IBC Section**: 306.3

---

## Group H — High Hazard

Buildings with materials that present a physical or health hazard.

### H-1 — Detonation hazard (explosives)
### H-2 — Deflagration hazard / accelerated burning
### H-3 — Physical hazard materials (flammable solids, oxidizers)
### H-4 — Health hazard materials (corrosives, toxic materials)
### H-5 — Semiconductor fabrication (HPM facilities)

- **IBC Section**: 307
- **Notes**: High-hazard occupancies have the most restrictive requirements.
  Quantity thresholds for hazardous materials determine if Group H applies.

---

## Group I — Institutional

Buildings where occupants are under restraint or incapable of self-preservation.

### I-1 — Supervised Residential (16+ occupants)
- **Uses**: Assisted living, group homes (>16), residential board and care,
  halfway houses, social rehabilitation facilities
- **IBC Section**: 308.3
- **Notes**: Occupants are capable of self-preservation but require supervision

### I-2 — Medical / Incapacitated Care
- **Uses**: Hospitals, nursing homes, psychiatric facilities, detox centers
- **IBC Section**: 308.4
- **Notes**: Occupants are NOT capable of self-preservation. Most restrictive
  institutional requirements.

### I-3 — Restrained (Detention)
- **Uses**: Prisons, jails, detention centers, correctional facilities
- **IBC Section**: 308.5

### I-4 — Day Care
- **Uses**: Daycare for more than 5 persons of any age, or children under 2.5 years
- **IBC Section**: 308.6
- **Notes**: Adult day care and child care for children under 2.5 fall here

---

## Group M — Mercantile

Buildings for display and sale of merchandise.

- **Uses**: Retail stores, department stores, markets, gas stations, drug stores,
  sales rooms, motor fuel-dispensing facilities
- **IBC Section**: 309.1
- **Notes**: Wholesale showrooms also fall under Group M

---

## Group R — Residential

Buildings for sleeping purposes (not institutional).

### R-1 — Transient Residential
- **Uses**: Hotels, motels, boarding houses (transient — occupants stay < 30 days)
- **IBC Section**: 310.3

### R-2 — Permanent Residential (3+ units)
- **Uses**: Apartments, condos (3+ dwelling units), dormitories, fraternities/sororities,
  monasteries, boarding houses (non-transient)
- **IBC Section**: 310.4
- **Notes**: The most common occupancy for multi-family residential projects.
  1-2 family dwellings are R-3, not R-2.

### R-3 — One- and Two-Family Dwellings
- **Uses**: Single-family homes, duplexes, adult care (5 or fewer), child care
  (5 or fewer within a dwelling)
- **IBC Section**: 310.5
- **Notes**: Most single-family homes. Also covers small-scale residential care facilities.

### R-4 — Residential Care (5-16 occupants)
- **Uses**: Group homes, assisted living (5-16 occupants capable of self-preservation)
- **IBC Section**: 310.6

---

## Group S — Storage

Buildings for storage of goods, products, and vehicles.

### S-1 — Moderate Hazard Storage
- **Uses**: Furniture storage, auto repair garages, lumber yards, tire storage,
  mattress storage, paper/cardboard storage
- **IBC Section**: 311.2

### S-2 — Low Hazard Storage
- **Uses**: Parking garages, aircraft hangars (noncombustible), metal parts storage,
  glass storage, food storage in noncombustible containers
- **IBC Section**: 311.3
- **Notes**: Enclosed parking garages are S-2. Open parking structures have
  their own section (IBC 406).

---

## Group U — Utility / Miscellaneous

Buildings not classified in any other group.

- **Uses**: Agricultural buildings, barns, carports, fences over 6 ft, greenhouses,
  retaining walls, sheds, tanks, towers, livestock shelters
- **IBC Section**: 312.1
- **Notes**: Catch-all for structures that don't fit elsewhere
