# IBC Fire-Resistance Rating Requirements — Reference

Source: International Building Code (IBC), Chapter 6 (Table 601, 602) and Chapter 7
Key tables: 601 (structural elements), 602 (exterior walls), 706 (fire walls),
707 (fire barriers), 708 (fire partitions)

---

## IBC Table 601 — Fire-Resistance Rating for Building Elements (hours)

| Building Element | I-A | I-B | II-A | II-B | III-A | III-B | IV-HT | V-A | V-B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Structural frame | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | HT | 1 | 0 |
| Bearing walls — exterior | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| Bearing walls — interior | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1/HT | 1 | 0 |
| Non-bearing walls — interior | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Floor construction (incl. beams) | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | HT | 1 | 0 |
| Roof construction (incl. beams) | 1.5 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | HT | 1 | 0 |

**HT** = Prescriptive heavy timber dimensions per IBC 2304.11 (not a rated assembly)

## IBC Table 602 — Exterior Wall Fire-Resistance by Separation Distance

Fire separation distance = distance from building face to closest lot line, centerline
of street/alley, or imaginary line between two buildings on the same lot.

### Fire-Resistance Rating Required (hours)

| Fire Separation Distance | Group H | Group F-1, M, S-1 | Group A, B, E, F-2, I, R, S-2, U |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 5 ft | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 5 ft to < 10 ft | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 10 ft to < 30 ft | 1 | 1 | 1 (or 0 if ≥15% openings allowed) |
| ≥ 30 ft | 0 | 0 | 0 |

### Allowable Openings in Exterior Walls (IBC Table 705.8)

| Fire Separation Distance | Max Opening % |
|---|---|
| < 3 ft | 0% (no openings) |
| 3 ft to < 5 ft | 15% |
| 5 ft to < 10 ft | 25% |
| 10 ft to < 15 ft | 45% |
| 15 ft to < 20 ft | 75% |
| ≥ 20 ft | Unlimited |

Openings in fire-rated exterior walls must be protected (fire-rated glazing or shutters).

## Occupancy Separation Requirements (IBC Table 508.4)

When a building contains **mixed occupancies**, fire barriers are required between them.
Two methods: **Separated** (fire barriers per table below) or **Non-separated** (most
restrictive requirements of all occupancies apply throughout).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | — | NS:1 S:1 | NS:1 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NP | NS:3 S:2 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:2 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:2 S:1 | NS:1 S:1 |

## Fire Walls (IBC 706)

A **fire wall** creates separate buildings for code purposes. Used to divide a large
building into smaller code-compliant portions.

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Rating | 2 hr (R-3), 3 hr (A, B, E, I, R-1, R-2, R-4), 4 hr (F-1, H, M, S) |
| Structural stability | Must be self-supporting (not dependent on adjacent structure) |
| Extends to | Roof level (with parapet per 706.6) or through roof |

---

## Fire Barriers (IBC 707)

Used for occupancy separations, shaft enclosures, exit enclosures.

| Use | Min Rating |
|---|---|
| Occupancy separation | Per Table 508.4 (see above) |
| Shaft enclosure (2 stories) | 1 hr |
| Shaft enclosure (3+ stories) | 2 hr |
| Exit stairway enclosure (≤4 stories) | 1 hr |
| Exit stairway enclosure (>4 stories) | 2 hr |
| Exit passageway | Same as required stairway enclosure |

---

## Fire Partitions (IBC 708)

Lower-rated assemblies for:

| Use | Rating |
|---|---|
| Dwelling unit separation (R-2) | 1 hr |
| Sleeping unit separation (R-1, I-1) | 1 hr |
| Corridor walls (where required) | 1 hr or 0.5 hr (per Table 1020.1) |
| Tenant separation in M or B | 1 hr |

---

## Common Fire-Rated Assemblies

| Assembly | Typical Rating | Common Construction |
|---|---|---|
| 1-hr wall | 1 hr | One layer 5/8" Type X gypsum each side of wood or steel studs |
| 2-hr wall | 2 hr | Two layers 5/8" Type X gypsum each side, or one layer on concrete/CMU |
| 1-hr floor/ceiling | 1 hr | 5/8" Type X gypsum ceiling on wood joists with specific insulation |
| 2-hr floor/ceiling | 2 hr | Two layers gypsum ceiling, or concrete slab with rated ceiling |
| 1-hr shaft wall | 1 hr | Proprietary shaft wall system (C-H stud, liner panel, face gypsum) |
